# MODUS FLOW · Brand & Art Direction

**Version:** 2026-11-11 · Applies to GitHub documentation, presentations, promo materials, game world art, avatars and future UI proposals.

## 1. Product positioning

MODUS FLOW is **first and foremost a workplace service** for employee feedback, accountable decisions, verified implementation and institutional knowledge. Its Great Commonwealth is a visual navigation and motivation layer, **not** an RTS game, a separate business workflow, or an alternative permission system.

- Primary message: «Сервис, который превращает обращения в решения, а решения — в организационный опыт».
- Secondary message: «Великое Содружество — визуальный мир реальных рабочих процессов».
- Never promote unimplemented 3D, real LLM, payroll insights or anonymous avatars as already working.
- Clearly label concept art vs real screenshots.

## 2. Human depiction: silhouettes only

**Mandatory:** no identifiable human faces, facial features, photos, realistic portraits, detailed eyes/noses/mouths, or likenesses of actual employees. This applies to the README, site hero, promotional screenshots, illustrations, GIFs, internal fantasy avatars and public presentations.

Allowed: anonymous silhouette figures from the back, faceless side-profile contours, clothed silhouettes, hands without identifying details, coats, helmets, armor, neutral icons, crests and abstract human-shaped markers.

Not allowed: stock portrait photography, AI-generated faces, named portraits in cards, fake employee identities, celebrity/actor likenesses, facial recognition aesthetics.

Actual product data such as employee names, departments and positions is controlled by backend access rights. A silhouette does not grant permission to reveal private information.

## 3. Art system

**Category:** premium cinematic corporate fantasy with architectural realism; elegant, mature, not cartoon.

- Mood: quiet grandeur, legible layouts, physical scale, restrained ceremonial gold.
- Palette: midnight navy #0C1524, graphite #131B2A, parchment #E9E2D5, muted gold #C6A06A, warm off-white #F6F3EC.
- Typography: elegant serif for identity-level headings; accessible high-quality sans for descriptions and controls. Do not embed unlicensed fonts.
- Buildings: real shape language, credible stone/wood/metal, believable roads and structure, nuanced lighting, no toy plastic materials.
- Motion: restrained fly-throughs and cinematic transitions; always support skip, reduced motion and static fallback.
- Silhouettes: modest size, meaningful composition, no detailed faces. Never make a person identifiable by their depicted body or apparel.
- UX: service call-to-action and actual work outcomes take precedence over decorative lore.

## 5. Identity and copyrights

Visual ambition may be compared to the craft of major game studios or minimalist hardware branding, but **never reproduce copyrighted UI, logos, characters, celebrity likenesses, or stock artwork without rights**. GIFs and images must never imply a larger budget or implementation scope than demonstrated.

## 6. Acceptance

1. On opening the README, it must be obvious within five seconds that MODUS FLOW is a **corporate service**.
2. Exactly distinguish current \`main\` demo, advanced working branch and planned 3D.
3. No human facial details visible anywhere in brand assets.
4. Animated image loads, loops and remains lightweight; static SVG remains legible.
5. Clear Russian and English copy; meaningful alt text; mobile-readable visual assets.
6. No modified application code, migrations or security rules in docs-only commits.
